Great to see ‘Beginner Boys’, the first single from the Minds forthcoming album ‘Sacrosanct’ selected as Record of the Week at Radio 2, where it’s also been A-listed. 

Sacrosanct releases in January of next year – and will be accompanied by a major tour celebrating the band’s five decades of incredible work. We’re proud to have been a part of that and it’s fantastic to see the next stage of the story take off with such success. 

<h2>Gilbert O’Sullivan – It’s Me</h2>

![](/assets/img/posts/Gilbert_O'Sullivan_Oct_2026.png)

Also in the news, iconic singer songwriter Gilbert O’Sullivan releases the song, ‘It’s Me’ from his latest album ‘On the Record’ – set to launch in November for the BMG label. Incredibly, it will be his 21st album. It’s been great working with him on his latest. A major UK and European tour is set to follow…

<h2>GiannaGold - Gianna Nannini</h2>

![](/assets/img/posts/Gianna_Nannini_Oct_2026.png)

My long-time collaboration continues with Italian rock legend Gianna Nannini – who is currently touring her ‘GiannaGold’ project. The shows have included a sold-out open-air symphonic concert at Berlin’s Waldbühne in front of 20,000 fans – and will take in the likes of Zurich’s Halenstadion as she tours in Europe.

Her latest release from the project is a track I produced for her entitled ‘Meraviglioso Errore.’

You can check it out here: <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSkANHlW97g">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSkANHlW97g</a>

<h2>Rebecca Poole – Album Launch</h2>

![](/assets/img/posts/Rebecca_Poole_Oct_2026.png)

Jazz FM favourite Rebecca Poole releases ‘Play On’ - the latest of the records I’ve had the pleasure of producing for her – and the album features several new songs from our many writing sessions together. 

Play On launches Nov 4th  at the Crazy Coq - with dates to follow at the 606 Club and Ronnie Scott’s. 

<h2>Keith Harkin – New Album</h2>

![](/assets/img/posts/Keith _Harkin_Oct_2026.png)

And finally, work is almost complete on Keith Harkin’s new record ‘This One’s For You, Kid’ – almost twelve years since we made our last one together! 

Keith’s been supporting Westlife in Ireland – also touring extensively in the States. More news on Keith’s record coming soon…
