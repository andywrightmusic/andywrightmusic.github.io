Whilst the album ‘Jeff’ was released some time ago now (August 2003), it feels like a good time to add some words about it here! This was the follow-up album to ‘You Had It Coming’ which was my first album with Jeff. It felt to him and to me that we had more creative offerings in our collaboration, so we embarked on what was to become ‘Jeff’. As always, after a successful collaboration, the question is ‘what shall we do differently this time?’ So we set about doing that. We did some live sessions in Metropolis Studio A with a different set of players: Steve Barney on Drums, Dean Garcia on Bass, and Jeff of course playing live. We achieved a lot in those sessions, but of course, most of the resulting sound was created by editing our best moments as we had done before. Later in the process, Apollo 440 came on board and produced two or three songs, and Dean Garcia (formerly of indie rock band Curve) collaborated on a couple of songs additionally to what he did on my sessions. I ended up with nine song productions out of the thirteen and a few co-writes to add in. My favourite moment would probably be hearing Jeff’s tear-inducing rendition of Bulgaria for the first time. This was one of Jeff’s ideas and is a guitar rendition of a Bulgarian choir piece that he had heard. We later added a full string orchestra, which was truly inspirational. Another Grammy for Jeff ensued!
